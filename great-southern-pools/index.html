Before you call

Questions we are asked.

Do you build new pools and renovate old ones?

Both. New builds and renovations are the two core services, alongside spas, water features, infinity edges and underwater windows.

Does an old pool need to be replaced, or can it be renovated?

An outdated pool doesn't need to be replaced to be transformed. With the right renovation, it can be reimagined into a modern, functional space that feels brand new — new interiors, coping, waterline tiles and reshaping.

What interior finishes do you offer?

Two options: fully tiled, for lasting durability and a refined, high-end look that resists fading and chemicals; or designer beadcrete with waterline tiles, for a balance of durability, low maintenance and modern style.

What happens once we sign on?

Four stages: a site inspection and design proposal, then approvals and planning (contracts, permits, engineering, scheduling), then construction — excavation, shell, plumbing, waterproofing, tiling and equipment — and finally filling, handover and a maintenance walkthrough.

Who will I actually be dealing with?

David and Dianne Moore, and David's son Michael. It's a family-owned business, and reviewers consistently name the three of them individually rather than just "the company."
