# Horgan Building & Renovations — Brief

## Who they are
Horgan Building & Renovations (HBR), based in Bomaderry NSW, owner-operated by
**Darryn Horgan**. 35 years in the building industry, 23 of them running his
own business (About text, horganbuildingandrenovations.com.au). Holds
Building Licence #299409C. Works Bomaderry and the surrounding South Coast —
extensions, renovations, new homes, outdoor living and granny flats, from a
single room to a whole house. Job size, per their own reviews, ranges from
three skylights in a hallway up to full home renovations and storm-damage
rebuilds.

## What customers praise (from their 5 Google reviews, all 5-star)
- **Reliability and finishing what he starts** — anna smallwood: "The entire
  build was smooth from start to finish, with no issues at all... Darryn was
  reliable, professional, and a pleasure to work with."
- **Problem-solving that saves the client money** — Matt Barnett: "Darryn is

- **Quality of finish on small jobs, not just big ones** — Margaret
  Dickinson: "The difference three skylights have made to our dull hallway

  efficiency of workmanship."
- **Long-standing trust** — Sue Cuninghame: "We have used HBR over the past
  20 years and have always found them to be incredibly professional,
  trustworthy and skilful."
- **Handling difficult/urgent jobs well** — peter maher: engaged HBR after
  storm damage to his house in April 2022; impressed "from the first
  encounter."

Themes: reliable and finishes the job, personally hands-on (not handed to a
site manager), skilled problem solver, good with both small jobs and large
ones, repeat/long-term clients.

## The one angle
**Owner-operated for 23 years, 35 years a builder — the person who quotes the
job is the person who turns up and builds it.** That is the one claim their
own About text makes and their reviews back up directly (Sue Cuninghame has
used them for 20 years; several reviews name Darryn personally, not "the
team" or "the company"). The headline used: *"Built by the man who quotes
it."*

## What's weak on their current site (for the call)
- Uses 3 generic stock/Getty photos (a UK brick-terrace extension with
  scaffolding, an empty display-home interior, a marble shower) alongside
  their own real work — undermines the "hands-on, local" story the text is
  telling.
- No suburb list anywhere on the site beyond "Bomaderry and surrounding
  areas" — a visitor from Nowra or Berry has no on-page signal that they're
  covered.
- The licence number (#299409C) is only in small footer/contact text, not
  used anywhere as a trust signal.
- Several of their own photos have real potential (a finished home-and-pool
  exterior, a wide-window living room addition with rural views) that don't
  appear to be used prominently.
- Platform is Duda; the site reads as a template with the text dropped in
  rather than built around this business specifically.
