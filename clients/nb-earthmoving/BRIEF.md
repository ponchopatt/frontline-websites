# Northern Beaches Earthmoving — Brief

## Who they are
Northern Beaches Earthmoving is a family-run excavation contractor based in Manly (53-55 Wood
Street, Manly NSW 2095). Jim Brigden is the owner and operator — he quotes, visits site and runs
the machine himself on every job. His wife Lynette runs the office and admin. Their own site says
they've served the local community "for over 25 years", and a separate stat block on the same page
says Jim personally has "21 years experience" as owner operator — both are stated explicitly and
not reconciled by them, so both are used, separately, rather than picked one over the other.
Phone 0422 929 660 (mobile), email jim@northernbeachesearthmoving.com.au. No ABN shown, no trade
licence number shown (excavation work of this kind doesn't require one in NSW the way building
does), no industry association or award mentioned anywhere on the site.

Services (11, all separate nav pages): rock sawing/rock excavation, swimming pool excavation,
driveways, retaining walls, under-house excavation, yard levelling/landscaping, difficult-access
excavation, fill and rubbish removal, detailed excavation, bulk excavation, sandstone block walls.

## The angle
**"The contractor other jobs turn out to be too hard for — Jim doesn't hand it off, he sorts it
himself."** Nearly every review independently mentions a hard site: heavy rock, tight or difficult
access, hand-dug excavation. Combined with the fact that Jim is the owner-operator on every job
(not a rostered employee, not a subcontractor), this is a genuinely different pitch from a
generic excavation contractor. The hero and headline lean on this rather than generic "reliable,
professional" language.

## What's weak on their current site (for Pat's call)
- No dedicated About or Team page — the owner/family story is buried in a few paragraphs on the
  homepage, easy to miss.
- Photos are almost all small WordPress web-optimised copies (500-800px), most from a 2016 upload
  batch; only a handful of images anywhere on the site reach full phone-camera resolution.
- No photo of Jim (or anyone's face) anywhere on the site — every photo is machinery, worksite or
  finished job. The demo's "owner" photo slot uses a branded truck/trailer shot instead of a
  portrait, because there is nothing else to use.
- Detailed hourly equipment rate card (rates page) instead of a simple job-price range — not
  useful for a first-time visitor trying to gauge cost, and not usable for this template's
  fee/build price lines (left blank, flagged in `_check`).
- No reviews, testimonials or social proof shown anywhere on the current site itself — all of it
  is only on their Google Business Profile, unlinked from the site.
- Meta title/description are generic "Excavation Contractors in Sydney" boilerplate, not
  distinctive.
