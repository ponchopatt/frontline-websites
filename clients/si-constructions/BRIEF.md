## Who they are
S&I Constructions Pty Ltd is a family-owned custom builder based in Mittagong, working across the Southern Highlands. They build custom new homes, extensions and renovations, decks and pergolas, and commercial buildings. They also draw plans in-house or work with the client's architect, and project-manage the build.

- **Over 45 years** in the Southern Highlands (their words; no founding year is published).
- **Jason Stokes**, owner and managing director, started as an apprentice with S&I in 1991.
- **Sarah** is named alongside Jason in reviews ("Jason and Sarah from S & I Constructions built our dream home"; their site testimonial says "Sarah has such a good eye for colour"). Her surname and role aren't published, so the page just says Sarah.
- **HIA member for over 25 years**, "fully licenced and insured", "over 1,000 successful projects completed".
- Office hours 7:00am to 5:30pm.
- **Job size**: from decks, pergolas and garage conversions up to architect-designed homes, a school gymnasium, medical centres and car showrooms. No prices are published anywhere.
- **Area**: "We proudly service the Southern Highlands region." Suburbs where they've built, from their own project pages: Bowral, Mittagong, Exeter, Burradoo, High Range.
