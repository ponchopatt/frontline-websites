# Nulook Pools — brief

## Who they are
A Canberra-owned fibreglass pool installer that sells, installs and maintains
in-ground fibreglass pools and does the landscaping, tiling and paving around
them, across the Canberra ACT region and surrounding NSW. Trading entity:
Culeld Pty Ltd T/A Nulook Pools, ABN 36 149 626 826 (contact page).

- **Proprietor: John Eldridge.** Homepage: "With nearly 30 years experience in
  the pool and landscaping industry, proprietor John Eldridge has the knowledge
  and experience that is essential to providing first class results for each
  project." (Note: the site was last updated 2022 per its sitemap, so
  "nearly 30" is the site's own wording — the demo uses exactly that, never
  "30+".)
- **Charissa** — not on the website at all, but named alongside John in 5 of
  the 8 Google reviews on file ("John and Charissa were amazing to deal
  with"). Surname and role unknown, so the demo only ever says "Charissa".
- Licences (contact page): Culeld Pty Ltd T/A Nulook Pools NSW Licence #
  236338C; John Eldridge ACT Licence # 20211046, NSW Licence # 91717S.
- Hours (contact page): "OPEN 7 DAYS WEEK / MON - SUN 6AM - 6PM".
- Phone 0411 055 329 (site header "Call John" and Google profile agree).
  Email office@nulookpools.com.au.
- Area (contact page): "the Canberra ACT region and surrounding NSW areas
  including but not limited to: Murrumbateman, Yass, Goulburn, Bungendore,
  Queanbeyan, Gundagai."
- Services: "SWIMMING POOL SALES, POOL MAINTENANCE AND LANDSCAPING" (footer);
  contact form options Pools / Landscaping / Maintenance. Pool installation
  page: "a complete package including full landscaping, tiling and paving

- Product: a range of 14 in-ground fibreglass shapes (Pool Range page), from
  the 4m x 2.8m Torrens flat-bottom exercise pool to the 13.5m x 5m
  Diamantina (dimensions read off the plan drawings on their own pages). The

  dealers (e.g. Rainwise Pools Melbourne).
- Job size: a residential backyard pool, with the paving and landscaping.

## The angle
**"From council approval to first swim — John and Charissa handle all of
it."** One outfit for the approvals, the dig, the crane, the paving and the
landscaping, with the same two people on the phone throughout. It is what
their own install page promises (one package, no separate trades, "onsite for
the whole process from start to finish") and it is exactly what reviewers
thank them for — especially the council part, which is the bit every pool
buyer dreads.

## What's weak on their current site (for Pat's call)
- The homepage hero and every big feature photo is an **iStock stock photo of
  people splashing** (kids in goggles, a baby in a float ring). Not one of
  John's pools on the front page.
- **No reviews anywhere** on the site, despite a perfect 5.0 from 35 Google
  reviews. Charissa, who customers name in almost every review, isn't
  mentioned at all.
- Pool Range pages are just photos with a "Button" label under each and no
  words — no sizes in text, no guidance on which shape suits which block.
- The 12-step install guide is printed twice on the same page, and the
  "PO0L MAINTENANCE" footer line has a zero for an O.
- Leftover template images (a CrossFit class, aerobics) still sit in the
  page code. Site last updated 2022.
- No mobile text button; phone number is the only fast contact.
