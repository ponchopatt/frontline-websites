(www.act-landscaping.com.au, read live with curl — it did not block us), the

## Who they are

A Fyshwick-based team of 14 that designs and builds whole high-end backyards
across Canberra and Queanbeyan — pools, sports courts, decks, pergolas, walls,
paving, turf and planting — with every trade managed in-house.

## Area

"Canberra, Queanbeyan, and surrounding areas" (home). Suburbs with evidence of

## Main services

Landscaping (garden design, softscaping, hardscaping); fibreglass pools (they
partner "exclusively with Harvest Pools"); sports courts (pickleball,
basketball half and full, tennis, multi-sport) and putting greens; paving
(driveways, paths, alfresco, pool surrounds); retaining walls (treated pine,
hardwood, concrete sleeper, block, stone, brick); turf and irrigation; plants
and gardens; decks, pergolas, fire pits, concreting, outdoor kitchens (showroom).

## Job size

Top end. The two featured projects are whole-property builds (Jerrabomberra:
court, putting green, pool, deck, pergola, sauna; Forrest: tennis court, pool,
pool house, veggie garden, designed by DSB Landscape Architecture). Their about
page says outright: "We don't offer quick patch jobs, low-cost shortcuts or
one-size-fits-all packages." The 1-star reviewer says they spent over $200k.
But they also take smaller work: Jayden Giugni's job was slab demolition and
site prep for a shed; Deatharra's was a retaining wall.

## What is weak on their current site — for Pat's call

1. **The award is buried.** "Award Winning" is the first thing on the home
   page, but nowhere does it say *which* award. The category, the year and
   Master Builders appear only inside photo file names and a framed
   certificate in one photo. The page never says "Master Builders 2025 winner".
2. **Template text from other businesses.** The Landscaping Canberra page has a
   form that says "We'll also include a personalized consultation to help you
   find the right heating and cooling solution for your space", another that
   says "I personally reply to every message within 1–2 business days" and
   offers "In-person at the clinic", and a "Book your Session" button.
3. **The paving page opens with turf copy.** "Paving & Retaining walls
   Canberra — Healthy lawns supported by professional turf and irrigation
   solutions", then "Explore some of our recent turf & irrigation projects".
4. **Typos in headings:** "turf & irragation" (every page), "Swimmng pools".
5. **No reviews anywhere on their site**, and a stock photo (a woman on the
   phone at a laptop, pexels-photo-5238258) on the contact block.
6. **"Unit 10/157 Gladstone St"** is the only local detail most people will
   see. The showroom — their best selling point after the award — is a
   separate page with no photo on the home page.
