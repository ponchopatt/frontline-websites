Written before the build, 2026-09-24. Every fact below is from their own site
(www.act-landscaping.com.au, read live with curl — it did not block us), the
Master Builders ACT awards results page, the ABN register, or
`data/google_reviews_top25.json`. Nothing is inferred beyond what those say.

## Who they are

A Fyshwick-based team of 14 that designs and builds whole high-end backyards
across Canberra and Queanbeyan — pools, sports courts, decks, pergolas, walls,
paving, turf and planting — with every trade managed in-house.

## Area

"Canberra, Queanbeyan, and surrounding areas" (home). Suburbs with evidence of
a real job: **Jerrabomberra** (award project), **Forrest** (their second

## Main services

Landscaping (garden design, softscaping, hardscaping); fibreglass pools (they
partner "exclusively with Harvest Pools"); sports courts (pickleball,
basketball half and full, tennis, multi-sport) and putting greens; paving
(driveways, paths, alfresco, pool surrounds); retaining walls (treated pine,
hardwood, concrete sleeper, block, stone, brick); turf and irrigation; plants
and gardens; decks, pergolas, fire pits, concreting, outdoor kitchens (showroom).

## Job size

Top end. The two featured projects are whole-property builds (Jerrabomberra:
court, putting green, pool, deck, pergola, sauna; Forrest: tennis court, pool,
pool house, veggie garden, designed by DSB Landscape Architecture). Their about
page says outright: "We don't offer quick patch jobs, low-cost shortcuts or
one-size-fits-all packages." The 1-star reviewer says they spent over $200k.
But they also take smaller work: Jayden Giugni's job was slab demolition and
site prep for a shed; Deatharra's was a retaining wall.
