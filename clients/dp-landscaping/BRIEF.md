# DP Landscaping & Design — Brief

## Who they are
Landscape design, construction and garden maintenance business based in Falls Creek, NSW,
run by owner Dale Pickering, with a team of ten (dplandscaping.com.au). Works across the
Shoalhaven — Nowra, Berry, Kiama, Kangaroo Valley and Jamberoo — for residential and rural
property owners, and alongside Sydney-based and local architects and construction firms.
Services, per their own site nav: Consultation, Garden Design, Construction, Garden
Maintenance. Job size ranges from small design/maintenance work up to "two large scale
landscaping jobs... in Kangaroo Valley" (Sam Brewer, Google review) — Tim Mason's review
calls them out as good "for a job of any size."

**Years in business: could not confirm, do not guess.** Their own about page says "Started
in 1996 by Dale Pickering" in one sentence, then "Dale's experience in the field of landscape
gardening spans the last 20 years" two sentences later — 1996 to 2026 is thirty years, not
twenty, so the two claims contradict each other and neither is used anywhere on the demo.
**Ask Dale directly for the real founding year or years of experience before this goes live.**

- **Reliable and hard-working on site.** Tim Mason: "on time honest and very hard working in
  sometimes difficult conditions."

## The one angle
**Dale's customers keep him for years — competitors can sell a one-off job, nobody else can
show a decade of repeat trust.** Two reviews prove it in their own words (15 years, 10 years),
and a third (Sam Brewer) shows repeat large-scale work. This became the hero headline
("The gardener Shoalhaven clients keep for years"), the "difference" statement section, and
one of the FAQ answers. Never turned into a company-age claim — it's about individual
customer relationships, which is what's actually sourced.
