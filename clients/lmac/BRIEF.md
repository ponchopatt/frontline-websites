# BRIEF — LMAC (Lower Mountains Air Conditioning)

Written before the build. Every factual line below is traced to a sentence on
lmac.com.au or to `data/google_reviews_top25.json`. Anything I could not trace
is in the FLAGS list at the bottom, not on the page.

## Who they are, in one line
Lower Mountains Air Conditioning (LMAC) sell, install and service domestic and
commercial air conditioning across Penrith and the Blue Mountains from a
walk-in showroom at Unit 2/129 Russell St, Emu Plains.

## Owner and key names
- **Andrew Strathdee** — Director / Sales Consultant. Their team page lists
  "Anthea and Andrew Strathdee"; the team grid lists "Andrew — Director / Sales
  Consultant". Reviews call him **Andy** ("Andy and the team at LMAC delivers a
  high quality service" — Shayne; "Thanks Andy, Anthea and the team at LMAC" —
  Kimberley Stapley). Their about text: "Andrew is a qualified electrician and
  experienced refrigeration, air conditioning and electrical technician."
- **Anthea Strathdee** — "Admin / Sales consultant / Accounts / Payroll".
- "Andrew and Anthea are responsible for the general running of the business
  including on-site sales and quotations."
- Team page also names: Sarah (Office Admin), Belinda, Amy, Dyson (Refrigeration

## Years

  have been operating since **1988**." (2026 − 1988 = 38 years.)

Their own words: "We are based in Emu Plains, proudly provide professional air
conditioner installation and servicing across **South Penrith, Blaxland,
Springwood, Kingswood, Glenbrook, Cranebrook, Katoomba, Glenmore Park and the
Blue Mountains**." Header strapline: "Commercial & Domestic Air Conditioning In
**Penrith & Surrounding Areas**". They also publish their own suburb pages for:
Leura, Jordan Springs, Castlereagh, Mulgoa, Blaxland, Glenbrook, Blackheath,
Windsor, Woodford, Glenmore Park, Emu Plains, Silverdale, St Marys, Kingswood,
South Windsor, Katoomba, North Richmond, Springwood, Richmond, Mount Druitt,
Agnes Banks, Bidwill, Blackett, Cambridge Gardens, Plumpton, Regentville, South
Penrith. No suburb on the demo comes from anywhere else.

## Main services
From their own nav and page copy:
- **Split systems** — "wall-mounted unit, a floor-mounted unit, or a ceiling
  cassette", with or without inverters.
- **Ducted systems** — "lies within your home's roof space"; zone controller
  "gives the option of up to 4 or 8 zones".
- **Multi-split** — "efficient cooling and heating for homes, offices, or
  multiple rooms while relying on just one outdoor unit".
- **Zoning solutions** (MyPlace / MyAir controllers).
- **Service, maintenance and repair** — "And service any brand, even if it
  wasn't our installation."
- **Air Clean Filters** — "We are the sole authorised stockist for Air Clean
  Filters in Western Sydney… high-efficiency G4, V-pleat disposable filters…
  25mm thick."
- **NSW Energy Savings Scheme rebate** — "LMAC is approved supplier for this
  scheme in the Penrith and Blue Mountains area."
- Brands: "We are proud to be dealers of six major brands: Mitsubishi Electric,
  Panasonic, LG, Daikin, Actron Air and Braemar."
- Licences: Contractor Licence No. 127921C · ARCTICK (CFC Purchaser) AU 16525 ·
  ARCTICK Handling Licence L008697 · Austel Licence 16079.
- Hours: "Monday – Friday: 8:30 am – 4:30 pm. After-hours service is available!"
- Seniors: "10% discount applies for general servicing only (Does not apply to
  parts required and/or repairs). Available only for seniors."

## Job size
Residential and commercial. A reviewer describes "three fixed units in my
mountains home"; another "replacement of our ageing A/C"; another "We have used
LMAC in both commercial and residential jobs". Their own timing guide: "Small
ducted air conditioning units OR wall-hung split systems can usually be
installed within a day; larger ducted system may require two days."
**No prices anywhere on their site.** None are invented on the demo.

## THE ONE ANGLE
**331 Google reviews at 4.8 — far and away the most-reviewed trade in this
queue — earned by the same family from the same Emu Plains shopfront since
1988.** Everything else (showroom, 14 staff, six brands, four licences) is
evidence for that one claim: this is not a ute and a phone number, it is a
38-year-old local institution, and the reviews prove it.

Hero headline comes straight from it: **"331 reviews. 4.8 stars. Since 1988."**

## What's weak on their current site (for Pat's call)
1. **The homepage is injected with SEO spam.** Several hundred words of
   casino/gambling link text in Finnish, Greek, Polish, Italian, Dutch and more
   ("RiseofBets.fi…", "plinko", "chicken road", "Our partner TowerrushCasino.cz")
   sit in the page body between the About section and the qualifications block.
   This is a hacked or sold-links WordPress install. It is invisible to a visitor
   but not to Google, and it is the single most urgent thing on their site.
2. **Live "Lorem Ipsum" on the Installation page**: "Lorem Ipsum is simply dummy
   text of the printing and typesetting industry…" under a heading about
   installation in Penrith.
3. **331 reviews and a 4.8 rating are invisible.** The homepage shows a
   Trustindex carousel where every review begins "Trustindex verifies that the
   original source of the review is Google." The number 331 appears nowhere.
   Their best asset is buried.
4. **Not one photo of their own finished work.** Every "suburb" page (21 of
   them) uses the same stock/AI split-system renders — beige panelled walls,
   European render-and-birch houses — while they have a genuinely great set of
   photos of their team, five branded vans and a lit showroom.
5. **Typos in body copy**: "Areas we searve", "3nd Year Refrigeration
   Apprentice", "The boys in stalling my new system".
6. **No prices or price guidance anywhere**, and no online booking — the form is
   a generic name/message box.
7. Copyright line says 2026; content is stale in places (2020/2023 staff photos
   alongside 2025 ones).
