# Utopian Landscaping and Paving Solutions — brief

Written before the build. Every fact below is either on their own website
(www.utopianlandscaping.com.au), in their published client letters, or in
`data/google_reviews_top25.json`. Nothing is inferred beyond what those say.

## Who they are

A family-run landscaping and paving outfit in Canberra that builds the whole
job themselves — walls, paving, decks, turf, irrigation and planting — so the
customer only ever deals with one business.

## Owner and key names

- **Derek** — the name on the front of their own site: *"Give Derek A Call On!
  0423 814 300"*. Named in seven of the eight Google reviews and in the client
  letters they publish, the oldest addressed "Dear Derek" and dated
  19 November 2007.
- **Aaron** — named in one Google review (Patrick Foley): *"Aaron was really
  helpful, turning my rough ideas into a practical reality. He was very
  committed, working through awful weather to get my landscaping done on time
  and on budget."* His role is not stated anywhere. **Do not describe him as
  anything but "named in the reviews".**
- Their site says "family run and operated". One published letter thanks a
  woman for "your hubby", which suggests a husband-and-wife business. Not
  confirmed — kept off the page, worth asking on the call.

## Years

Not stated anywhere on their site, and no ABN registration date to hand. The
only hard date is the earliest client letter they publish: **19 November 2007**,
from John & Val of Macquarie ACT. Letters then run through 2009, 2012, 2014,
2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020. The page says "2007 — earliest client letter" and
nothing more. **No "years in business" number is claimed anywhere.**

## Area

Canberra, Queanbeyan, Yass "and all of ACT surrounding areas" (their words,
homepage and contact page), and their contact page adds it "is not limited".
Suburbs named in their own published letters: **Macquarie**, **Holder**,
**Belconnen**. Their postal address is PO Box 5032, **Chisholm** 2905 ACT.
No street address is published.

## Main services

Straight off their homepage, in their order:

- Retaining walls and feature walls — stone, brick, sleeper, corten steel
- Paving, pool coping and paving, garden edging, concreting
- Decks, pergolas, fencing, privacy screens
- Rockeries, garden beds, native planting
- Irrigation, turf, synthetic grass, lawns, garden lighting
- Water features, ponds
- School playgrounds and sensory gardens

## Job size

"Covering both small and large landscaping projects from a small courtyard to
a rural property" (their homepage). The reviews bear it out at both ends:
Michael Forsythe's was "a small paving job… a soggy dying townhouse lawn" and
Charlie Stephenson's took in "rockeries, paving, turf, irrigation and other
features". Their biggest published job is a school sensory garden and
playground with a boardwalk, sandpit, roly-poly slopes, stepping stones and
artificial turf. No prices anywhere — nothing on the page claims one.

## THE ANGLE

**You do not need a plan. You need an idea — they work out how to build it.**

It is the one thing their site, their tagline and their reviews all say at
once. Their logo reads *"Only Limited By Your Imagination"*. Their homepage
says they build "based on your designs and ideas, providing the expertise to
reproduce your plans and ideas". And their customers say it back unprompted:
rough ideas turned into a practical reality, receptive to our ideas, good ideas
of their own, integrated our native garden into the new landscaping.

Hero headline comes straight off it: **"Rough ideas turned into real gardens."**
(No comma — a line-ending comma in the display face drops its tail onto the
line below.)

The support under it is the second true thing: they do every part of the job
themselves, so there is one quote, one crew and one person to ring.

## What is weak on their current site — for Pat's call

1. **The homepage title tag says "Building Surveyor For Government Contracts |
   ACT".** That is what Google shows and what the browser tab says on a
   landscaping business's homepage. The About, Testimonials and Contact pages
   all share one title too: "Our Premium Services Are Ideal For Multiple
   Projects | ACT". This is the single most embarrassing thing on the site and
   the easiest opener.
2. **Three stock photos sit among their own work** — a close-up of hands
   potting marigolds, a North American flagstone-and-daylily border, and a
   European hosta garden with granite setts. They own 140-odd genuine photos of
   Canberra jobs and are showing someone else's garden.
3. **Their gallery is served at 560px.** Every one of ~150 gallery images is
   scaled down to 560px wide when the originals on their own CDN are 1920px.
   The work looks smaller and softer than it is.
4. **Twenty-five five-star Google reviews appear nowhere on the site.** The
   testimonials page is a slider of pasted letters, several undated in the
   carousel, the newest from 2020, each headed with the unedited template text
   "Slide title" and a dead "Button". The About page has three more "Slide
   title / Write your caption here / Button" placeholders that were never
   filled in.
5. **A broken Instagram embed** from 13 September 2019 is still on the About
   page, with the leftover plugin credit "Powered by embedinstagramfeed
   gmailbulkemailcom" printed underneath it.
6. **No phone link in the body copy, no SMS, no enquiry form above the fold.**
   The number is there, but a customer on a phone has to read and dial it.
7. **The address in circulation is http://** — that one does 301 to https and
   the site sends HSTS, so it is not insecure, but anything printed, written on
   a van or linked from an old directory still reads as "not secure" to a
   customer who types it. Worth a mention, not a scare.
8. Footer reads "Website by Melbourne IT". Built on Duda. Nobody has touched
   the copy in years.
