# Sunset Pools — brief

Written before building. Every fact below is from **their own site**
(www.sunsetpools.com.au, read through the Wayback Machine — the live site sits
behind a Cloudflare challenge our IP cannot clear) or from
`data/google_reviews_top25.json`. Nothing here is invented, and anything that
could not be confirmed is marked **ASK BEN**.

---

## Who they are

A Sydney-only concrete pool builder and renovator who takes the jobs other
builders call too hard — rooftops, indoor pools, pools built into a building's
structure, and renovations on sites you can barely get a wheelbarrow into.

- **Owner:** Ben Thompson, founder and Managing Director. Their about page is
  written entirely around him; every one of the six curated Google reviews names
  him. Instagram handle is `benthompsonsunsetpools`.
- **Years:** their site says "for over 20 years" and "20+ years of local
  experience" in several places. No founding year is published — **ASK BEN**.
- **Area:** strictly Sydney metropolitan. Their own FAQ names Northern Beaches
  (Manly, Mosman, Palm Beach), Eastern Suburbs (Bondi, Vaucluse, Coogee),
  North Shore and Inner West, and the Sutherland Shire (Cronulla, Miranda).
  Project photos add Bellevue Hill, Wahroonga, Killara, Gordon, Lindfield,
  Willoughby, Northbridge, Greenwich, Neutral Bay, Hunters Hill, Woolwich,
  Putney, Drummoyne, Balmain, Lilyfield, Strathfield, Burwood, Kensington,
  South Coogee, Sans Souci, Alexandria, Wentworth Point, Parramatta, Oatlands.
  Head office: 12/3 Clyde St, Rydalmere NSW 2116.
- **Main services:** pool renovations; new residential concrete pools; rooftop /
  on-springs / part-of-building-structure pools; commercial pools and water
  features; hydraulic consulting (schematics, plant room layout, filtration,
  lighting, heating, energy costs, in-floor cleaning, structural engineering);
  3D pool design.
- **Job size:** no prices are published anywhere on their site — **ASK BEN**.
  What is published: renovations "most restorations take 3 to 6 weeks", and
  award categories they have won in, which run from "construction under $50k"
  through "construction over $100k" to "commercial construction over $250k".
  Commercial clients named on the site include a Sydney harbour hotel rooftop,
  Western Sydney University at Parramatta, and apartment/strata complexes.
- **Licence:** "licensed NSW Swimming Pool Builder with Business License No.
  166547C" (their own FAQ, and the homepage footer).
- **Phone:** 1300 000 412 (their site and their Google profile agree).
  **Email:** info@sunsetpools.com.au (mailto on their awards page).
- **Members of:** SPASA, Master Builders Association, APSP.
- **Featured in:** Sydney Living, Smart Home Ideas, SPASA Magazine 2009–2012,
  and the book *AquaTekture — International Award-Winning Pools, Spas, Water
  Environments III*.
- **Awards (their own awards page):** SPASA chapter, state and **national**
  medals from 2009 to 2016 — including national golds for Innovation and for
  Sustainable, national gold for construction over $100k, and national gold for
  commercial renovation over $250k. MBA golds 2011–2014. GAVA "International"
  2012: silver for an indoor lap pool, bronze for an infinity-edge pool.

## The one angle

**The pool other builders called too hard.** Ben Thompson takes the sites that
stop everyone else — on a roof, inside a building, down 132 steps, with a crane
over the house — and engineers the parts you never see (structure, hydraulics,
plant room) himself.

It is their own positioning, in their own words, buried on an inside page:
"Where many pool builders might consider non-standard pool projects 'too hard',
Sunset Pools takes architects' and engineers' more challenging ideas in their
stride." The reviews say the same thing from the customer's side (cranes, tricky
site, complex renovation, consultant for an unconventional indoor pool), and the
national **Innovation** medals are the industry saying it too.

The hero headline comes from this: *The pools other builders call too hard.*

## What is weak on their current site (for the call)

1. **A visitor cannot get in.** The live site sits behind a Cloudflare challenge
   that never clears for us. If that is happening to customers or to Google, it
   is costing them enquiries. This is the opener.
2. **Ben is invisible above the fold.** The homepage hero says "Multi-Award
   Winning Sydney Pool Builder / For over 20 years we've been creating statements
   of luxury" — four rotating slides, all with the same words. The single
   strongest asset they have (Ben, personally, on hard jobs) is three clicks
   deep on the About page.
3. **Nothing on the page is theirs alone.** "Statements of luxury", "Where Dreams
   Meet Excellence", "quality of materials", "we keep to timelines", "cost
   transparency" would fit any pool builder in Australia. Meanwhile the 132-step
   Bellevue Hill job, the cranes, the indoor lap pool and the national
   Innovation medals sit unused.
4. **No reviews anywhere.** 4.7 from 25 Google reviews, and not one of them is
   on the site. There is not even a link to the profile.
5. **"over 500+ happy clients"** on the homepage is exactly the kind of round
   number a sceptical buyer discounts, and it is the only number they lead with.
6. **The awards page is a spreadsheet.** Forty-odd rows of Gold/Chapter/State
   with a "View Project" link on each, and no photo beside any of them.
7. **Two marketing agencies in one footer** ("Powered by MLK Marketing",
   "Website by Buzz Web Media", plus a "Powered by Tap Out Marketing" line in an
   older copy) and a "Reputaion FAQs" page title with a typo in it.
8. **Phone is the only fast path.** No SMS, no sticky call button on mobile, and
   the enquiry form asks for a street address before it asks what you want.
