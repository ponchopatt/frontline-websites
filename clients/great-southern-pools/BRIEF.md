# Great Southern Pools — brief

## Who they are
A family-owned concrete pool building and renovation company based in Greater
Sydney, run by David and Dianne Moore, with over 30 years of experience in the
pool building industry (gspools.com.au homepage and /about-us-1: "With over 30
years of industry experience, David and Dianne Moore lead a family-owned pool
building company known for quality workmanship, personalised service, and
attention to detail."). David's son Michael also works in the business —
named alongside David and Dianne in three of their Google reviews (Paul
Giovenco, Kath, Maureen Antuch, data/google_reviews_top25.json).

Trading name on their logo and Google Business Profile: "Great Southern Pools
& Renovations." Their own site nav and page title use the shorter "Great
Southern Pools," which is what this demo uses throughout.

## What customers praise (from Google reviews, data/google_reviews_top25.json)
- **Honesty and professionalism of David personally** — Kath: "David Moore was
  the epitome of honesty and professionalism."
- **Reliability and communication through the job** — Paul Giovenco: "Good
  advise, reliable and on time"; Kath: "reliable and answered all of our
  questions along the way and after our pool was completed."
- **Craftsmanship / finished quality** — Daniel Moore: "Excellent
  craftsmanship, quality pools"; Chance P: "Outstanding quality of work."
- **Value for money** — Lachlan W: "reasonable price for the quality of
  work."
- **On time / on budget** — Chance P: "Work completed ahead of schedule and
  under budget."
- Several reviewers name David, Michael and Dianne individually rather than
  just "the company," which reads as a genuinely small, hands-on outfit.

Google rating: **4.7 stars, 12 reviews** (data/google_reviews_top25.json). One
1-star review (8 years old) says the reviewer was not shown "member of the
Pool & Spa Association" status and disputes their contract terms — this demo
does not claim any association membership, and doesn't repeat or rebut the
dispute.

## The angle
**"The Moore family has been building Sydney's pools for 30 years — David,
Dianne and Michael, all still on the tools."** Two generations of one
family working the job personally is the one thing this business has that a
sales-rep-and-subbies outfit can't claim, and it's said in their own reviews
independently by three different customers, not just on their marketing.

## What's weak on their current site
- No ABN, licence number, warranty or insurance wording anywhere on the site
  — a pool builder page with zero credentials is unusual and worth asking
  David to fix.
- No suburbs or service-area detail beyond "Greater Sydney" — no way to tell
  a Cronulla customer from a Parramatta one that they cover their area.
- No reviews or testimonials on the site at all, despite a strong 4.7/12
  Google record.
- Generic, auto-generated alt text on every photo ("Modern backyard pool with
  clear water and glass safety fencing") — none of their genuinely striking
  photos (the harbour-view pool especially) are captioned or contextualised.
- Two different phone numbers in circulation: the site's own header/footer
  and contact page both show 0413 513 572, but their Google Business Profile
  lists 0418 603 656. This demo uses the number published on their live site
  now; flagged for Pat to confirm which is current with David before this
  goes out.
- No FAQ page, and the "Features and design" nav item is only an anchor link
  on the New Builds page, not its own page — easy to miss.
