A Canberra builder of extensions, renovations, knock-down rebuilds, new homes, multi-unit developments and bathrooms. The office is at 51 Townshend St, Phillip (Woden). "With over 30 years in the building industry, our team has…" They work with your architect or designer, and a dedicated construction manager runs each job with site meetings and weekly progress reports.

## What's weak on their current site (for the call)
- The phone number and name are inconsistent across the web: Google says "Prostyle Homes", the site title says "ProStyle Building Group", and our list had another number. The old domain's `www.` no longer resolves at all, which breaks old links and business cards.
- No email address and no hours. The only contact route is a Squarespace form.
- "Award winning" appears three times with no award named. Houzz lists MBA and HIA awards (Custom Built Home, Green Living, HIA Professional Builder of the Year) but their own site never says which, so we couldn't use them.
- The About page has no words about the team: no Josh, no Craig, no years, just a sponsorship paragraph and an uncaptioned crew photo.
- The portfolio has 12 projects titled only by suburb ("Campbell" three times). No job type, no story, no "extension vs new build".
- The photos are uploaded small: nearly everything is 1000px or 966px wide, and only the Garran shoot is 2500px. On a big screen they're soft.
- The testimonials on the site are initials only. Their Google rating (3.5 from 19) sits below their site's claims.
