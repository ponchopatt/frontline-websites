Written 2026-09-24, before building, from omara.com.au. I read every page with curl: home, the six service pages, Other Construction Types, Granny Flats, Duplexes, Steel & Metalworks, Tips, The Next Step, Our Valued Team & References, Optional Upgrades, Contact and Kellyville. Google data is from `data/google_reviews_top25.json`.

## Who they are
O'Mara Constructions Pty Ltd is a family-owned custom home builder in its third generation. The office and showroom are at Unit 1, 103 Railway Road North, Mulgrave, near Windsor in the Hawkesbury. They build custom new homes, second storey additions, extensions, renovations, kitchens and bathrooms, granny flats, duplexes and multi-unit developments. They also have their own steel and metalwork shop.

- **Founded 1964** by **Barry O'Mara OAM**. The OAM was for service to the building industry. He was President of the MBA NSW in 1987–88, a Grade 1 arbitrator, and a building disputes referee for the Department of Consumer Affairs.
- **Luke O'Mara** runs the company day to day. He is a director and holds a B. App. Sc (Building) with Honours from UTS (1994). The team page lists "Qualified supervisors, Lic No: Q14472S".
- **Family on the job**: Luke's sons **Dallas and Colby** are carpenters, which makes three generations. Luke's father-in-law **Garry** is an on-site working foreman. **Sharon** (Luke's wife, a director and the company secretary) and **Yvette** (Evans, Luke's cousin) run the office.
- MBA NSW members since 1968, as their site states on every page.
- Job size runs from a bathroom up to multi-unit developments. The budget options on their enquiry form go from under $50k to $1M+. We don't publish any prices.
- **Area**: they name Pitt Town, Wisemans Ferry, Katoomba, Rouse Hill, Kellyville, Hornsby, Berowra, Parramatta, Blacktown and Penrith. They charge the same rates in every area, with no extra for travel. Project captions add Wilberforce, Kurrajong, Leura, Mt Wilson, Turramurra, St Ives, Mosman, Vaucluse and more.
- **Phones**: office (02) 4577 4912, which matches Google. Luke's mobile is 0419 979 804. The site says "Call & speak to the builder direct – Luke O'Mara", so the demo leads with Luke's mobile, and that also switches on the text button.

## What customers praise
Google shows **5.0 from 2 reviews**, and one of the two has no text. The only review we can use is Lauran Trevena's: "Great honest and friendly builder. Provides great work and a great price." Themes: **honest, friendly, good work, fair price**. That's too thin for a reviews section, so the proof comes from facts on their own site:
- 60+ years and three generations, with the founder an OAM and a past MBA NSW President.
- 8 homes built for the Athletes Village at the Sydney 2000 Olympics, under Mirvac Lend Lease Village Consortium.
- Flame-zone (BAL FZ) bushfire homes at Hornsby and Spencer, pole homes on slopes at Kurrajong and Wisemans Ferry, flood-affected blocks at Leonay and Penrith, and heritage work on buildings from as early as the 1820s.
- Their own full-time carpenters do everything from framing to fix-out joinery. They carry $8,000,000 a year of HBCF cover, and their standard contract is the MBA BC4 fixed price contract.

## The one angle
**Any block, any design, since 1964.** Their own words are "We build on any site," "We build projects that other builders won't or can't" and "We love difficult jobs, on difficult sites." Most custom builders in the Hills and Hawkesbury can't show flame-zone homes, pole homes on steep bush blocks, heritage work and Olympic Village homes all from one family firm. The hero shows the Blue Mountains bushfire home at dusk: a lit glass box over a steep bush block. The headline is "Any block. Any design. Since 1964." The statement section, "We love the difficult blocks", uses the Central McDonald bushfire home.

## What's weak on their current site (for Pat's call)
- The home page is one very long wall of text, set partly in Comic Sans ("You name it, we build it !"). There are 60 years of great credentials, but they're buried halfway down.
- Barry's OAM and MBA presidency, and the Olympic Village homes, sit in plain paragraphs. There are no headline trust signals.
- On a phone, the call button is the office landline. Luke's mobile, the number the site itself says to use ("speak to the builder direct"), is harder to find.
- Some of their best photography is professional twilight shots: the Wilberforce extension, Turramurra, the stone home at Wilberforce and the Blue Mountains bushfire home. These are shown as small thumbnails in grids of 4 and never used big.
- A few gallery images are Google Street View grabs (Cammeray, Hunters Hill, South Windsor) with the Street View arrow still visible.
- The site has typos ("Arc Deco", "Bunglaow", "midical", "Leichhdart", "Wilbeforce"), and the team page contradicts the home page on whether Dallas and Colby are apprentices or qualified tradesmen.
- The enquiry form asks for a budget band and documents before any human contact, which puts people off.
- There's no Google reviews presence: just 2 reviews in 60 years. That's an easy talking point.
