Good to know

How much does a split system cost in Canberra?
It depends on the room, the system and where the outdoor unit goes — which is exactly why we quote on site rather than over the phone. The quote is free either way.
How long does an install take?
A single split system is usually a straightforward day. Ducted takes longer — we'll give you a realistic timeframe as part of the quote.
Do you service what you install?
Yes, and we'll also service or repair a system someone else installed.
How often should I service my system?
Once a year is the general rule, more often for a system that runs hard through both a Canberra summer and winter. Regular servicing is also what keeps a workmanship warranty valid.
Do you do commercial work?
Yes — HVAC install, repair and maintenance, mechanical installation, ventilation and cool room installation for commercial and industrial sites.
What areas do you cover?
Canberra and Queanbeyan, including Gungahlin, Belconnen, Woden, Weston Creek, Tuggeranong, the Inner North and South, Molonglo, Jerrabomberra and Googong.
What warranty do I get?
A 12-month workmanship warranty on every install, on top of the manufacturer's own warranty on the equipment itself.
Which brands do you install?
Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Fujitsu, Toshiba, Samsung and Temperzone.
