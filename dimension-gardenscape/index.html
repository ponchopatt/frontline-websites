How it runs

From first meeting
to finished garden.

The design and quoting stage runs two weeks to a month depending on complexity. You see the concept plan before anything is booked in.

01

Initial meeting

We discuss your ideas, your lifestyle and use for the garden, and the style you prefer. Our designer checks site constraints and opportunities, and talks through your budget.

02

Sketch plan

A sketch plan showing the main elements of the design, followed by a second meeting for your feedback on the layout, plant species and materials.

03

Final plan & quote

The final concept plan, showing all plants and hardscape details, with any staging discussed and a quote prepared.

04

Construction

Our team builds it — site prep, structural work, paving, then topsoil, planting, turf, irrigation and mulch. A booking fee secures your place in the schedule; a materials deposit is due four weeks before construction starts, with weekly progress payments during the build.

Before you call

Questions we're asked.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. Once we have a rough idea of the garden design you're after, we can put you in touch with previous clients with similar projects, or you can view our work on this page.

How much will it cost?

Every garden is different, with many different components and costings. Call us on 02 6297 9040 for a free quote.

How long will my project take?

Design and quoting typically runs two weeks to a month, depending on the complexity of the project — a bespoke water feature or engineered retaining wall needs a more detailed design and quote than a small garden refresh. Construction time depends on the size and scope of the job, plus material availability and weather.

Who actually does the work?

Our own team of experienced, qualified landscapers and designers — including qualified carpentry for decks and pergolas. Where a project needs another trade, we coordinate with a solid network of specialists such as electricians and plumbers.

Are you on site, or in an office somewhere?

In Trevor's words: “As Chief Landscaper, I spend most of my time on site, however if I am needed elsewhere on the day I am easily contactable anytime.”

Can I do some of the work myself to save money?

Yes — a lot of our quotations can be changed to supply-only, letting you take on parts of the work yourself. Many clients choose to handle the planting and mulching.

How does billing work?

A booking fee secures your place in our schedule, then a materials deposit is due four weeks before construction starts so we can order materials ahead of time. Progress payments are charged weekly during construction, followed by a finalised bill. We accept direct deposit, cash, cheque and online card payment.
