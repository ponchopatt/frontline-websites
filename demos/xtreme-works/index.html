General

What services does Xtreme Works provide?

We specialise in residential, commercial and industrial concreting, covering excavation, formwork, reinforcement, placing, finishing and after-care.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our team is fully licensed, insured and compliant with Australian Standards.

Do you work on both small-scale and large-scale projects?

Absolutely. From small footpaths to large industrial slabs, no job is too big or small for us.

What areas do you service?

Greater Sydney, Western Sydney, the Blue Mountains and surrounding regions.

What is the process to book a quote?

To book a quote, residential clients can schedule a site inspection. Commercial and industrial clients should send their plans to: AJK.xtremeworks@hotmail.com

Cost, quotes & payments

How much does concreting cost?

Costs vary based on size, access, excavation needs, reinforcement, finish type and site conditions.

For accurate pricing:

  • Residential: Book a site inspection
  • Commercial/Industrial: Send plans

Email AJK.xtremeworks@hotmail.com

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, we offer free, no-obligation quotes.

Do you offer fixed pricing?

Yes, once we complete a site inspection or review your plans, we issue a fixed quote outlining all inclusions.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, staged progress payments and invoices for commercial/industrial works.

Do you require deposits?

Yes. A small deposit may be required to secure your booking and materials.

Residential projects

Do you do driveways and pathways?

Yes—driveways, footpaths, patios, alfresco slabs and extensions.

Can you remove old concrete?

Yes. We can demolish and dispose of existing concrete.

Can you help me choose a finish?

Absolutely. We assist homeowners in selecting colour, design and texture options.

Commercial & industrial projects

Do you work to engineered plans?

Yes. Email all drawings and documents to AJK.xtremeworks@hotmail.com.

Do you coordinate with builders, engineers, and site managers?

Yes, we regularly collaborate with builders, engineers, and site managers to ensure seamless project execution.

Do you provide pour schedules and risk assessments?

Yes. We can supply pour plans, SWMS and required documentation

Can you complete large pours or multi-stage slabs?

Yes. We have experienced crews for large industrial slabs, hardstands, warehouses and suspended decks.

Timelines & scheduling

How quickly can you start?

Start times depend on current workload, weather and project size. We provide an estimated schedule after inspection or plan review.

How long does a typical job take?

Small residential works may take 1–2 days, while commercial/industrial slabs may require multi-day preparation and coordinated pours.

Can you work after hours or weekends?

Yes. For commercial and industrial sites, we can arrange night works, weekend works or shutdown coordination as required.

After the job

Will you clean up the site after completing the job?

Yes, we ensure all jobs are left clean and tidy, with all rubbish removed, so you don't have to worry about the cleanup.

Will you provide after-care instructions for my concrete?

Yes, we provide detailed after-care instructions, including curing, sealing, washing, and long-term maintenance to ensure your concrete lasts.

Do you provide warranties?

We do not offer a formal written warranty; however, we stand by our workmanship. If any defect arises that is caused by our work, we will promptly attend to it and rectify the issue as soon as we become aware of it or are notified.

Site preparation & drainage

Do you handle excavation?

Yes. We manage all excavation and disposal of spoil.

Do you install reinforcement?

Absolutely—we install mesh, bar chairs, dowels and any engineered reinforcement required.

Can you fix drainage or level issues?

Yes. We can build falls, install spoon drains, pits, or improve water runoff to prevent pooling.

What if my site has limited access?

We assess access during inspection. If needed, we organise concrete pumps and compact equipment.

Finishes & curing

What types of finishes do you offer?
  • Plain concrete
  • Coloured concrete
  • Exposed aggregate
  • Broom-finish (non-slip)
  • Stamped or stencilled
  • Burnished/steel trowel finish
  • Machine trowel finish (industrial)
What is the curing time for concrete?

Concrete curing times are as follows: Walk on: 24–48 hours, Light vehicles: 7 days, Full strength: 28 days.

Do you seal the concrete?

Yes. Sealing is recommended and can be included in your quote.

Safety & compliance

Do you follow Australian Standards?

Yes. All works comply with AS3600 (Concrete Structures) and relevant codes.

Do you provide SWMS and insurances?

Yes—for commercial/industrial projects, we can supply all compliance documentation upon request.

Are your workers trained?

Yes. Our team holds the required tickets, induction cards and qualifications.
