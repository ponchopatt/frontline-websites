24 hr

Burst pipe or leak

Do this now:
  1. Turn the water off at the main stop tap. It's usually next to your water meter, near the front of the property.
  2. If water is near power points, lights or the switchboard, keep away and don't touch anything electrical.
  3. Don't stand under a wet or sagging ceiling.

Blocked drain or toilet

Do this now:
  1. Stop using water in the house (taps, shower, washing machine) and don't flush again.
  2. If it's backing up inside or outside, keep kids and pets away from it.

No hot water

Do this now:
  1. Check the hot water system is switched on at the power point or switchboard, or that your gas is on. If a switch keeps tripping, leave it off.
  2. Don't take any covers off. Text us a photo of the label on the unit (the sticker with the model number) — it helps us work out what it needs.

24 hr

Gas smell

Do this now:
  1. Get everyone outside.
  2. On the way out, open doors and windows, and turn the gas off at the meter or bottle if it's safe.
  3. Don't touch light switches or power points, and don't use lighters or matches.
  4. Once you're outside, away from the smell: if anyone is hurt or in danger, call 000.
  5. To report a gas leak, call the 24-hour gas emergency line on 13 19 09. Then call us.
