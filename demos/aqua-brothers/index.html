Do this now: stop running water and don't flush again. If it's backing up outside, keep kids and pets away.

Do this now: check the unit has power or gas. Don't open it up. Text us a photo of the label on the side and we'll know what we're dealing with.

Smell gas

Do this now: don't use switches, phones or flames near the smell. Turn the gas off at the meter if it's safe, open doors and windows and get everyone outside. If the smell is strong, call 000 first. Then call us from outside.
