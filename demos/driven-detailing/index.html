The fine print

Driven+ includes one detail per calendar month at member price, for one nominated vehicle, on a standing monthly booking. Extra details in the same month are charged at the normal price. Pay on the day; no lock-in, cancel anytime before your next booking. Each month’s detail counts as one visit on the rewards timeline. Missing a month resets your rewards progress and ends Gold status (one holiday pause per year). The rewards timeline repeats every year. Driven+ Maintenance starts with a Signature Detail at member price if your car hasn’t been detailed by us in the last 6 weeks. If your package already includes a reward, you can swap it for an enhancement of equal value. Rewards have no cash value and can’t be transferred.

How long does a detail take?

Maintenance takes 45 to 60 minutes, Signature 1.5 to 2 hours and Prestige 3 to 4 hours. Signature Interior is about 1 hour and Prestige Interior 2 to 2.5 hours. Very dirty cars or added extras can take longer.

Do you come to my home or work?

Yes. Seb details your car in your driveway, carport, garage or work car park. He just needs a water tap and a power point.

Is there a travel fee?

Outside Wantirna South, yes. Text your suburb to 0404 714 415 for an exact quote. Driven+ members pay no travel fee within 10 km of Wantirna South.

What’s the difference between Signature and Prestige?

Signature is a complete clean inside and out: wash, wheels and arches, door jambs, full vacuum, dash and trims, leather shampoo and glass. Prestige adds clay bar, carnauba wax, premium tyre dressing, deep carpet shampoo, steam cleaning, stain and odour treatment, and leather conditioning.

Do you do paint correction and scratch removal?

Yes. Light scratch removal starts from $30 and full paint correction from $250, depending on the paint and the size of the car. Send a few daylight photos of the paint for an exact quote.

Do you do ceramic coating?

Yes. The Spray-On Ceramic Coating is $60 and can be added to any detail. It’s a ceramic (SiO2) sealant that adds gloss, makes water bead off and keeps the car easier to clean. It’s a spray-on layer, not a multi-year professional ceramic coating.

How does Driven+ work?

One detail a month on a standing booking, at 10% off. You get free upgrades the longer you stay, and Gold status (15% off) after 12 visits. Pay on the day, no lock-in.
