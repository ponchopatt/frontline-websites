How we detail the outside of your car

  1. Wheels first. Rims, tyres and arches are cleaned before the paint, so brake dust and grime aren’t splashed onto a clean panel.
  2. pH-balanced wash. A premium pH-balanced shampoo that lifts dirt without stripping existing protection.
  3. Door jambs, wipers and glass. The places a drive-through wash misses.
  4. Microfibre dry. Soft towels only: no chamois or squeegees that drag grit across the paint.
  5. Decontaminate and protect. Clay bar to remove bonded contamination, then a spray-on ceramic sealant for gloss and water beading.

What does a clay bar do?

Even after a wash, paint can feel rough to the touch. That roughness is bonded contamination (industrial fallout, tree sap, overspray and road tar) that shampoo alone can’t lift. A clay bar glides over lubricated paint and pulls those particles out, leaving the surface smooth so the ceramic sealant bonds properly and the finish looks deeper.

A clay bar is part of every Exterior and Full Detail.

Spray-on ceramic sealant

Every Exterior and Full Detail finishes with our spray-on ceramic sealant. It’s a ceramic (SiO2) sealant sprayed onto clean paint that adds slickness and gloss, makes water bead and sheet off, and makes the car easier to wash between details.

A spray-on ceramic is a quick, affordable layer of protection applied as part of your detail. It isn’t the same as a multi-year professional ceramic coating, which needs full paint correction and hours of curing. It goes on after the clay bar, so it bonds to perfectly clean paint.
