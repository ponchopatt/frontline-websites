Careful detailing for Wheelers Hill cars

Wheelers Hill is only a few minutes from our Wantirna South base, so it’s right on our doorstep. We detail European and prestige vehicles, and our process is built for them: pH-balanced shampoo that won’t strip existing protection, wheels cleaned first, soft microfibre drying, and leather that’s shampooed, not wiped with an all-purpose cleaner. Add Leather Conditioning to finish it.
