Before you call

Questions we get asked.

Do you build the pool yourselves, or subcontract it?

Ourselves. Antony holds a pool builder's licence as well as a landscape contractor's licence. That is unusual — most landscapers subcontract the pool, which is where the finger-pointing starts when the pool and the garden don't meet properly. Here it's one contract and one team.

How long does a project take?

It depends entirely on scope, and we'd rather tell you honestly after seeing the site than give you a number now. What we can promise is that we run one job at a time, so once we start, we stay until it's done. One client's terrace garden — paving, fencing, a gate, lighting, irrigation and planting — was finished in three weeks.

Do you handle council and DA approval?

Yes. If your project needs council approval, we manage that process as part of the design phase so you're not chasing it yourself.

What does the design cost?

After the first consultation you get a Design Fee Proposal, priced to the size and complexity of your project rather than a flat rate. You approve that before any design work begins, and the construction quote comes later, built on the approved design.

Will you work with my architect or builder?

Often, yes — a lot of our work sits alongside a renovation or a new build. Bring us in earlier rather than later. One client's only regret in their review was not involving us sooner in their build.

Do you do small gardens, or only large projects?

Both. We produce a full landscape design for every project regardless of size — courtyards and terraces included. Some of the work we're proudest of is small.
