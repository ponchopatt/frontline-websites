Before you call

Questions we're asked.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Falls Creek and work across the Shoalhaven — Nowra, Berry, Kiama, Kangaroo Valley and Jamberoo. If you're nearby but not on that list, call and ask.

Do you design gardens as well as build them?

Yes. Consultation and garden design come first, so what gets built matches what you asked for — and we work with your architect or builder if you have one.

Will you look after the garden once it's finished?

Yes — garden maintenance is one of our core services, and several clients have kept us on for years after the original job. One client has been with us for ten years, another for fifteen.

What size jobs do you take on?

Both large and small. Reviewers mention two separate large-scale jobs in Kangaroo Valley, and others describe smaller design and maintenance work — "highly recommended for a job of any size."
