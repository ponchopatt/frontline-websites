Before you call

Questions we are asked.

What does a consultation cost?

Face-to-face consultations, drafting and sourcing work are charged at $300 (inc. GST) an hour with a senior designer, or $150 (inc. GST) an hour with a junior designer. Commercial packages are available after an initial consultation. All pricing quoted is RRP.

Do you handle new builds, or only renovations?

Both. New Builds and Renovations and Developments are both on our services list, alongside Floor Plan Redesign for existing homes that just need the layout fixed.

Will I have to source materials and suppliers myself?

No. We're a one-stop solution studio — there's no legwork for you to do. We work with you right from the blueprint phase, and our strength is in offering materials that aren't visible on the market elsewhere.

Do you only do homes, or commercial work too?

Both. Commercial and Hospitality Interior Design are on our services list, and we've worked on multi-residential common areas too — David Murray's review describes us designing the refurbishment of his apartment complex's foyer in Miranda.

Can you help with just furniture, blinds or upholstery — not a full renovation?

Yes. Furniture curation, our Blinds & Shutter Studio, custom upholstery and our in-house fabric library are each their own service, separate from a full interior design project.

How experienced is Toni?

Over 40 years in the design industry. She's an Interior Designer F.D.I.A. — a Fellow of the Design Institute of Australia — and serves the industry as a DIA Counselor. Her interior architecture and construction training lets her resolve complex floor plan issues herself.
