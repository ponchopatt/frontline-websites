What we build

Extend, rebuild
or start
new.

Our project managers and builders work with your architect or designer, and our construction team looks after the build.

01

New homes

We work with your architect or designer on a home that's modern, functional and personal to you. The final calls on finishes and fittings are yours, and our construction team looks after the build.

02

Extensions & renovations

Expand or change your home to suit how you live now. We work with your designers on the drawings and approvals, and finish the outside to flow with the house you have, so it doesn't look added-on.

03

Knock-down rebuilds

Start from a clean slate on your own block. Our project managers and construction team look after the entire process, and we want your input on every part of the design.

04

New developments

Multi-unit developments in established suburbs and on new blocks. We can run the feasibility study, talk with the neighbours, and look after planning, documentation and lodging for approval.

05

Bathrooms

Our interior designers and experienced tradesmen work together on a bathroom that works well and uses modern colours and finishes. Many of our bathroom clients came to us by word of mouth.

Before you call

Questions we get asked.

Can you work with our own architect?

Yes. Our project managers and builders work with your architect or designer. We can also help from the design stage through working drawings and approvals.

How will we know how our extension is going?

On an extension or renovation, a dedicated construction manager runs the programme and keeps you updated, with site meetings and weekly progress reports.

Can we live at home during a renovation?

A family in Waramanga lived on site through a major renovation with us.

Will an extension look tacked on?

It shouldn't. We finish the outside to flow with your existing house, so it looks sharp and fresh rather than added-on. And we build to current building and energy requirements.

Do you build multi-unit developments?

Yes, in established suburbs and on new blocks. We can run feasibility studies, handle consultation with neighbours, and look after the planning, documentation and lodgement of plans with the authorities.
