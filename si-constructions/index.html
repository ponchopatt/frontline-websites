We lead a team of local contractors to finish your home in a timeframe set at the start of the project, and we work to your budget.

Before you call

Questions we get asked.

Can you build from our architect's plans?

Yes. If you already have architectural plans, we'll work with your designer so the home is built the way it was drawn. Our High Range home was designed by a local architect around the owners' established gardens.

We don't have plans yet. Can you draw them?

Yes. We drew the plans for our Mittagong custom home ourselves, to fit the size and shape of the block. If you'd rather use an architect, we work closely with a number of designers and can recommend one.

Who does the work on site?

Jason and our local subcontractors. We support local businesses first, and bring in materials and trades from further afield when a job needs them.

Do you take on smaller jobs?

Yes. As well as new homes and commercial builds, we do extensions, renovations, decks and pergolas.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're a fully licensed and insured building company, Builder's Licence No. 30559, and we've been HIA members for over 25 years.

Can we come and see you?

Yes. Our office is at 28 Tyndall Street, Mittagong, open 7:00am to 5:30pm. We're also happy to meet you on site.
