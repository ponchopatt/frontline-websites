What's included in a detail.

Five steps before anything is called finished. Tap one to watch it.

  1. 01Prewash

    A thick layer of foam sits on the paint and lifts the loose grit off before anything touches it.

  2. 02Touch wash

    Two buckets, a clean mitt and pH-neutral shampoo, one panel at a time, rinsing after every pass.

  3. [Iron decon clip, 9:16. Not in the repo yet]

    03Iron decon

    A chemical that reacts with the iron thrown off your brakes and turns deep purple as it dissolves it.

  4. [Clay bar clip, 9:16. Not in the repo yet]

    04Clay bar

    Clay glides over lubricated paint and pulls out what's bonded into it: overspray, rail dust and sap.

  5. 05Ceramic sealant

    The protection goes on last, panel by panel, and cures hard onto the clear coat.
