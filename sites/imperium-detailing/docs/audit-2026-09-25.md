# Imperium site audit — 2026-09-25 (read-only)

Four checks on a local build that matched the live homepage word for word: looks (AI-slop, polish), motion, bookings (conversion and copy), and accessibility, mobile, speed and SEO. Skills used: audit-ai-design-slop, better-interface, review/improve-animations, apple-design, landing-page-design, form-design, better-writing, accessibility-audit, responsive-design, plus Lighthouse and axe.

**Note:** `public/media/steps/` is missing from this repo, but it is live (it returns 200). So the live site is deployed from a newer copy or another repo. Make fixes there, not here, until that's sorted.

## Fix first (costing leads)
1. **Quote form "sent" message lands off-screen on phones.** `booking-form.tsx:38-40` only scrolls for fallback/error. Scroll to the sent panel and focus its heading. S
2. **"Text us" buttons do nothing on desktop.** Price guide "Lock it in by text" (`price-guide.tsx:154`), footer (`footer.tsx:52-57`), CtaBand (`cta-band.tsx:217`). Use the QuoteCta pattern (text on phones, /book/?service= from md). S
3. **Intro curtain.** Hides the hero 1.7s on desktop and ~3s on phones, most of the hero animation plays behind it, and it stays blank with JS off (`intro.tsx`). Remove it and let the hero be the intro, under 0.9s. S
4. **Page wipe on every link click.** About 1.2s of black (`page-wipe.tsx`). Remove it, or use a 200ms crossfade that never hides the page. S
5. **Home is slow on mobile.** Lighthouse 69, LCP 5.1s, 2.9MB. The hero webm preloads (1.8MB, even for reduced motion), 11 below-fold posters load at start, and posters are 720w shown at 321. Start the video after load, lazy-set posters, add 480w posters, drop the intro. M
6. **Hero text over bright video.** Unreadable at 768–900px, grey H1 lines ~2:1 on phones, and "CANBERRA."/"ONE." run 30–60px onto the video at 1440 (`hero.tsx:98-135`). Keep copy off the video until lg, add a scrim, make lines white on phones. S–M
7. **Hero "Get a quote" stuck 12px low.** CSS transform transition fights GSAP `from({y:12})` (`hero.tsx:76,155,161`). Add `clearProps:"transform"` or drop the transition. S
8. **Page H1s hidden until JS** (`section-heading.tsx:18-22` data-reveal). Delays LCP on /book/ (3.7s). On phones, the /book/ form starts below the fold. Don't reveal-animate H1s; put the form first on mobile, with the rating. S
