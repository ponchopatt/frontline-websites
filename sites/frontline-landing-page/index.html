Most detailers don’t have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem.

Anyone can spend $2,000 on Meta. Whether that money turns into jobs is decided in the first hour after someone asks for a price.

You’re on a job when they message.

Your hands are full of polish. You reply at 6pm. By then they’ve booked whoever answered first. Businesses that reply within an hour are nearly 7 times as likely to get a real conversation going as ones that wait longer.1

One text isn’t follow-up.

Lots of people don’t book from the first message. They need a nudge a day later, then a few days after that. Nobody can remember to do that for 100 leads a month while they’re detailing.

You can’t see your numbers.

Which ad brought in the ceramic coating? Who still owes you? What did you make this week? If you can’t see it, you can’t put more money into what works.
