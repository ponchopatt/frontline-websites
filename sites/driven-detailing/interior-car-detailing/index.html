Interior car detailing at your home or work.

A properly clean cabin is the part of the car you actually live in. Driven Detailing brings a full interior detail to your driveway or workplace car park anywhere across south-east Melbourne: vacuum, plastics, leather, glass and odour, done by hand.

Quick answer: Driven Detailing’s mobile Interior Detail starts from $140 for a hatch or sedan ($165 for an SUV or ute, $170 for a 4x4 or 7-seater), and takes about an hour. A very dirty cabin can be up to $60 more, and Seb tells you before he starts.

What customers say

Need more than a reset?

The Interior Detail is a thorough reset: crumbs, dust, fingerprints on the screens, scuffed door cards and leather that’s lost its clean feel.

If there are spills or stains in the carpet or seats, or a lingering smell, add Stain Removal, a Steam Clean or Deep Odour Removal. Steam lifts grime from vents, cup holders and seat rails that a vacuum and wipe can’t reach.

Not sure? Text a few photos of the cabin and we’ll tell you honestly what will get the result you want.
