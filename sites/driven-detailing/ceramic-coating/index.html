Ceramic coating.

A ceramic coating is a hard, glossy layer bonded to your paint. Dirt sticks less, washing is quicker, and the paint is protected from sun and bird droppings for years, not weeks. Seb polishes the paint first, then coats it.

What a ceramic coating does

  • Dirt sticks less, so the car stays cleaner for longer.
  • Washing is quicker. Water beads and sheets off, taking the dirt with it.
  • The paint is protected from sun, bird droppings and tree sap for years, not weeks.
  • The gloss lasts. A polished, coated car keeps that deep, wet look.

What it won’t do

  • It won’t make the car scratch-proof. Keys and bad washing can still mark it.
  • It won’t stop stone chips. That’s a job for paint protection film.
  • You still need to wash it. It just takes less time.

If anyone promises you more than that, ask questions.

How Seb coats your car

  1. Wash and decontaminate. A full wash, then a clay bar to pull out bonded grime.
  2. Polish. A single-stage machine polish takes out swirl marks and haze. A coating seals in whatever is underneath, so this comes first.
  3. Coat. The ceramic coating goes on panel by panel and is levelled by hand.
  4. Cure. The car stays dry and undercover for 24 hours while the coating hardens.

A coating needs a dry, covered space for the day. Text Seb on 0404 714 415 to sort out where.

3, 5 or 7 years?

Choose by how long you’ll keep the car. Selling in a couple of years? 3 years covers it. Keeping it for the long haul? 7 years means one coating for most of the car’s life with you.

Looking after it

  • Wash with a pH-neutral car shampoo and a clean mitt.
  • Skip automatic car washes: the brushes leave swirl marks.
  • A regular Maintenance Detail, from $165, keeps it looking its best.
