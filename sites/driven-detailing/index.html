How long does a detail take?

A Full Detail takes 2 to 2.5 hours, an Interior Detail 1.5 to 2 hours, and an Exterior or Maintenance Detail 45 to 60 minutes. Very dirty cars or added extras can take longer.

Do you come to my home or work?

Yes. Seb details your car in your driveway, carport, garage or work car park. He just needs a water tap and a power point.

Is there a travel fee?

Outside Wantirna South, yes. Text your suburb to 0404 714 415 for an exact quote.

Why is the price a range?

Every car is different. A full or interior detail on a car in normal shape is the first price. A very dirty car can be up to $60 more. Seb tells you the final price before he starts. Exterior and maintenance details are set prices.

Which detail do I need?

The Full Detail is a complete clean inside and out: wash, clay bar, spray-on ceramic sealant, wheels and arches, door jambs, full vacuum, dash and trims, leather shampoo and glass. Interior and Exterior Details do one half of that. Maintenance is quick upkeep for a car that’s already in good shape. For more, add steam cleaning, stain removal and more from the add-ons, or book a Paint Correction for swirl marks and scratches.

Do you do paint correction and scratch removal?

Yes. Paint correction on a hatch or sedan is $347 for a single-step and $547 for a two-step. Bigger cars, and light scratch removal, are quoted. Send a few daylight photos of the paint for an exact quote.

Do you do ceramic coating?

Yes. A ceramic coating starts at $997 for a hatch or sedan with 3 years of protection, and you can choose 5 or 7 years. The paint is polished first, then coated. See the prices for every car on the ceramic coating page. Every Full and Exterior Detail also includes a spray-on ceramic sealant, a lighter layer that adds gloss and makes water bead off.
