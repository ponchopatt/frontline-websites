Paint correction and scratch removal.

Swirl marks, light scratches and dull, hazy paint make even a clean car look tired. Paint correction machine-polishes the clear coat to remove those defects and bring back a sharp, glossy reflection, done at your home or workplace across south-east Melbourne.

What paint correction fixes

  • Swirl marks: the fine circular scratches you see in sunlight, usually from automatic car washes or dirty towels.
  • Light scratches and scuffs that sit in the clear coat.
  • Dull, hazy or oxidised paint that has lost its gloss.
  • Water spots and light etching from hard water or bird droppings that weren’t removed quickly.

What it can’t fix

Paint correction works by removing a very thin layer of clear coat to level out defects. If a scratch has gone through the clear coat to the colour or primer (a good test is whether it catches your fingernail), polishing can soften how it looks but won’t remove it. Those need touch-up or panel repair. We’ll always tell you honestly what’s achievable before we start.

Our paint correction process

  1. Wash and decontaminate. A full wash, then a clay bar to remove bonded contamination, so we’re polishing clean paint.
  2. Inspect. We check the paint under good light and agree on the target finish with you.
  3. Machine polish. A machine polisher and compound or polish matched to your paint to remove the defects.

Single-step or two-step?

A single-step correction is one machine polish. It takes out swirl marks and haze, and brings back the gloss.

A two-step correction cuts first, for deeper defects, then polishes to refine the finish. Not sure which your car needs? Send photos and Seb will tell you.
