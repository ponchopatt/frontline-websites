# Film List: what we borrowed, and how we do our own version

A short study of four tools, so Imperium Film List takes the useful ideas without copying
anyone. **How it was studied:** their public websites and public help docs only, as a normal
visitor. No logins, no paywalls, no private APIs, and none of their code or designs. (The
reverse engineering toolkit is built for taking apart app files and code. The rules for this
job rule that out, so it wasn't used on these products.) Sources are listed at the end.

## HeyOrca: Inspo Library

What it does (from its help docs): a per-calendar library of saved Instagram and TikTok posts.
You save from the phone's share button or by pasting a link, add a note, and file it in a
collection. Items can be filtered by platform and marked "used".

| Worth borrowing | Our version |
|---|---|
| Save from the phone's **share button**, or by **pasting a link** | An iPhone Shortcut on the share sheet posts the link straight into our Inbox. Pasting works too. |
| A **note** saved with each link ("why we like it") | The same: one optional note per saved reel. |
| **Filter by platform** (Instagram, TikTok, other) | Inbox filter chips: All, TikTok, Instagram, Not used yet. |
| **Mark as used** once content is made from it | Done for you: "Make it ours" links the new idea to the reel, and the reel shows "Made into: …". |
| Saving works even when the app it came from is fussy | The link is always saved first. The TikTok thumbnail is a bonus that's fetched after. |

## Foreplay: swipe file

What it does (from its site and help docs): a swipe file of saved ads, organised into boards
and tags, shareable by link, with transcripts and AI analysis on top.

| Worth borrowing | Our version |
|---|---|
| **Save the reference once, keep it for good** | Saved reels live in the Inbox until you delete them, even after they're used. We keep the link and our notes only, never the video file. |
| **Tags** for filtering later (UGC, review, before/after) | Free tags on every idea (hook idea, B-roll idea, trend, skit…), plus our 13 fixed **types**. Filter by both. |
| **Team view**: see what everyone saved | One shared inbox and board for the whole crew. |
| **Transcript + AI analysis** of a saved ad | Nice-to-have 8: paste a caption or transcript and get our own version back (hook, shot steps, caption) in Imperium's style. |

## Trello: boards, cards, reminders

What it does (from Atlassian's docs and guides): lists of cards on a board, with members, due
dates, reminders, checklists and label filters.

| Worth borrowing | Our version |
|---|---|
| **Lists as stages**, cards moving left to right | Five stages: Inspo, To film, Filmed, Edited, Posted. One big button moves a card on, with an Undo. No dragging, which is hard on a phone. |
| **Members + due dates** on a card | Each idea gets one person and a "film by" date. |
| **"Due today / overdue" filters** | The crew's whole app is a Today screen: only their videos due today or overdue. |
| **Checklists** | The shot steps (one per line) show as a list the crew can tick through while filming. |
| **Due-date reminders** | A reminder link GoHighLevel can call each morning, which returns "Ant, 2 videos today: …". |

## Sandcastles: hook breakdowns

What it does (from its public site and third-party reviews): finds short-form videos that
outperformed in a niche, breaks down why they worked (hook, storytelling, format), and helps
write your own script from proven structures.

| Worth borrowing | Our version |
|---|---|
| **Break a winning video into parts**: hook, story, format | Every idea has its own hook, shot steps, caption, people needed and length, so a reel becomes a plan. |
| **"Make it yours" from a proven video** | The "Make it ours" button turns a saved reel into an idea, with the link attached for the crew to watch. |
| **Script from the breakdown** | Nice-to-have 8 (AI breakdown), in our caption style: statements not questions, 😭 or 💀 where it fits, no "book now" or prices, 12 to 20 seconds, our own audio. |
| **Learn what performs** | Nice-to-have 10: views on posted videos, and average views by type, so we film more of what works. |

## What we left out on purpose

- Saving or downloading the videos themselves. It breaks Instagram's terms, so we keep the link
  and our own notes only.
- Calling Instagram for any data. An Instagram reel is a link and a "Watch the example" button.
- Discovery feeds, competitor tracking and trend libraries. They're big features we don't need yet.
