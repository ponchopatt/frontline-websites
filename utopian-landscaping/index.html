Before you ring

Questions we get asked.

Do I need a design before I ring?

No. If you have plans drawn up, we build to them — that is exactly what we do. If all you have is a rough idea and a yard you are sick of looking at, that is enough to start with. Bradley Wood's review puts it plainly: “receptive to our ideas as well as communicating well to us on ideas of their own”.

Do you take small jobs, or only big ones?

Both. Anything from a small courtyard to a rural property. One of the reviews on this page is a townhouse lawn that was soggy and dying and is now tightly paved; another is a native garden worked into a new landscape with rockeries, paving, turf and irrigation.

Do you do the whole job, or subcontract parts of it out?

The whole job. Walls, paving, decking, fencing, screens, turf, irrigation, lighting, concreting and planting are all done by us, so you only have to deal with the one business.

Do you do school jobs?

Yes. School playgrounds and sensory gardens are on our list. We built a school sensory garden and playground with a sandpit, roly-poly slopes, stepping stones and artificial turf, and the children wrote the thank-you notes for that one themselves, Year 2 right through to Year 6.

Where do you work?

Canberra, Queanbeyan, Yass and the ACT surrounding areas. It is not a hard boundary, so if you are a bit further out than that, ring and ask.
