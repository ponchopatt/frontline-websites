What we do

The whole yard,
not one part of it.

The hard parts and the green parts. Retaining walls, decks and paving; turf, garden beds and the drainage underneath them. Water features and garden lighting as well.

01

Garden construction

Garden beds, edging, paths and planting. We can be as involved in the design as you want — a little or a lot.

02

Retaining walls

Sandstone block, brick, besser block or timber sleeper. Terracing a sloping block so you get back ground you can use.

03

Timber decking

Natural hardwood decks, with help picking the timber for the sun, shade and rain it will get. Pergolas too. One customer had Trent replace their deck and turf the nature strip.

04

Paving & driveways

Clay pavers, natural stone, travertine and concrete. Driveways, pool surrounds, paths and entertaining areas — and small repair jobs.

05

Lawns & turf

Levelled, graded and laid on a proper turf underlay, because a lawn is only as good as the ground under it. Back yards, front lawns, nature strips.

06

Drainage & irrigation

Stormwater drainage so the wet corner stops being wet. Drip irrigation that can run off rainwater or recycled water, split into zones.

Before you call

Questions worth asking.

Do you come out and quote?

Yes, and it costs nothing. Book the job and we will organise a free on-site quotation.

How small a job will you take?

Small is fine. We take large and small scale projects, residential and commercial. One of our Google reviews is for a replaced deck and a turfed nature strip.

Our side yard floods. Can that be sorted before anything is planted?

Yes. One customer's review describes us listening to their concerns about a side area flooding, then putting in extra drainage pipes and bigger river rocks and a better design around it. Another lists identifying and resolving drainage issues among the work on their job.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Licence 157365C. We carry public liability and workers' compensation, and we are eligible for Builders Warranty Insurance. We are also current members of the Housing Industry Association and the Landscape Association NSW & ACT.

Where do you work?

The Hills District, north-west Sydney and the Sydney metro area, from a Castle Hill base. If you are not sure whether you are in range, ring Trent and ask.

Do you do the whole yard, or just one part of it?

Either. Paving and driveways, pool surrounds, garden design and construction, irrigation and water features, pergolas, retaining walls and excavation work. One Google review describes a single job that covered drainage, garden mulching, steel edging and welding, a drip sprinkler system and earthworks using machinery.

How long have you been going?

We are a family business, established in 1987 — over thirty years' experience. In 2004 we won the Landscape category in the Australian Achiever awards for Excellence in Customer Service.
